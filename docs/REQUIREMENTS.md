# Requirements Tracker

## How to read this tracker

The authoritative source is the case PDF; `reference/CASE_BRIEF.md` is its verified working transcription. Classifications are **mandatory**, **recommendation**, **permission/assumption**, or **evaluation criterion**. ADC requirements are kept separate from repository working rules in `AGENTS.md`.

Statuses begin as **pending implementation** unless the item concerns documentation or source inspection completed in Stage 0. “Planned” is not verification. Evidence will be linked only after an observable check.

| `workplace_policy.pdf` | 2 | Current-looking 2025 policy replacing earlier guidance; contains detailed location, device, and reimbursement rules. |

Relevant findings: parsers must preserve headings, lists, tables, dates, versions, identifiers, and page/source provenance. Retrieval/evaluation must exercise contradictions, supersession, cross-document questions, and answer abstention—not merely happy-path similarity. No OCR-only sample was found; scanned-PDF behavior remains outside the verified corpus unless added later.

## Ambiguities and unresolved questions

- **A-001 — `conversation_id` creation contract (resolved for prototype):** `/upload` requires a caller-provided ID, reuses it when present, and creates that ID when new (DEC-011).
- **A-002 — “last 5 messages” (resolved for prototype):** use the five latest individual role messages in chronological order; the current question is separate (DEC-019).
- **A-003 — `/chat` JSON schema and citation shape (resolved for prototype):** return conversation ID, status, answer with numbered markers, and structured filename/one-based-page citations (DEC-019).
- **A-004 — document scope (resolved for prototype):** uploaded documents are scoped by conversation (DEC-011); Stage 3 must enforce the metadata filter during retrieval.
- **A-005 — indexing completion semantics (resolved for prototype):** upload is synchronous and confirms success only after vector-count verification and relational finalization (DEC-012).
- **A-006 — source precedence/conflict behavior (resolved for prototype):** explicit supersession may establish a relationship; otherwise conflicting relevant sources are disclosed and cited rather than resolved from date alone (DEC-019). Enterprise governance remains Stage 6.
- **A-007 — citation granularity (resolved for prototype):** return numbered inline markers plus structured PDF filename and one-based page citations (DEC-019).
- **A-008 — model/network expectations (resolved for prototype):** embeddings run locally; chat uses hosted Gemini Flash with an environment-only key. Live cost/latency and organizational data-sharing approval remain open; free-tier prompts may be used by Google to improve its products (DEC-020).
- **A-009 — non-functional thresholds:** “Robust,” latency, quality, scalability, security, and availability have no numeric targets. Prototype targets and enterprise SLOs must be proposed and labeled as assumptions.
- **A-010 — cloud context:** A cloud-native choice is encouraged, but preferred provider, existing Apex contracts, region/data residency, security controls, and budget are unknown.
- **A-011 — deliverable medium:** Repository/ZIP and presentations are required, but due date, slide template, presentation length, and submission mechanism are not supplied.
- **A-012 — PDF support boundary (resolved for prototype):** text-readable PDFs up to 10 MiB are supported; encrypted, malformed, and text-empty PDFs are rejected; OCR and multilingual guarantees are excluded.
